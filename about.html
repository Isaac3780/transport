About CJS Lines

Our journey began humbly. Our commitment is to serve. Our ambition is to grow together.

Humble Beginnings in Singapore

CJS Lines started as a small Singapore logistics operation with a simple belief: every customer deserves honest, reliable service — no matter the size of the shipment.

From coordinating a few local deliveries, we gradually grew into a trusted partner for sea freight, road freight, export coordination and warehousing support. We are still a tight-knit entrepreneurial team, and that personal touch remains at the heart of everything we do.

We listen, we respond, and we find practical solutions. Our success is measured by the relationships we build and the cargo we deliver safely, on time.
